# EIP/ERC Repository Boundary

EIP standards are maintained in `ethereum/EIPs`; ERC standards are maintained in `ethereum/ERCs`. New ERC proposals and amendments belong in `ethereum/ERCs`, not this repository.

This repository may retain historical references or publication artifacts only where their archival or redirect status is documented. Such material is not an invitation for new ERC edits.

| Content | Canonical repository | Canonical URL | Change location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EIPs | `ethereum/EIPs` | `https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-<n>` | `ethereum/EIPs` |
| ERCs | `ethereum/ERCs` | ERC publication site, if applicable | `ethereum/ERCs` |
| Historical ERC material | Explicitly documented archival or redirect content | Documented archival policy | No new edits here |

Shared infrastructure is being migrated toward a versioned reusable workflow and a machine-readable manifest. Until that migration is complete, the existing merge adapter is transitional and its retirement criterion is adoption of shared workflow version 2 by both repositories.
